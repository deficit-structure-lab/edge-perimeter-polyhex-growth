# Provenance records

This directory preserves dated research-history and reproducibility records associated with
the polyhex growth project.

These files are historical records. Statements such as "pending", "not yet used by the
paper", or similar status language describe the state of the project at the date of the
record and do **not** necessarily describe the current status of the paper.

The provenance materials are not mathematical evidence for the theorems in the paper.
Mathematical claims are supported by the proofs and computations in the paper and ancillary
files.

## Contents

- `FREEZE_RECORD.md` — dated freeze/status record.
- `LEDGER_NOTE_provenance_chronology.md` — chronology of the proof routes and editorial
  promotion history. It records that the initial author-directed proof route is the
  one-cell deletion proof now preserved as companion Note B.
- `skeleton_note.tex`, `skeleton_note.pdf` — audited standalone record of the
  shortest-side skeleton lemma at the time it was isolated.
- `proof-reachability/` — archived experiment prompts and, when available, run records.
